When motivation disappears

Read this instead of negotiating with yourself.

01 / IDENTITY

You don't need motivation.

You need a rule. Training is an appointment. Some sessions will suck. Show up anyway.

02 / CONSISTENCY

Never miss twice.

One missed workout is life. Two is a pattern. Return without punishment.

03 / WHOLE BODY

Legs and abs are not optional.

You are not building a torso on sticks. Squat, hinge, carry, brace and run.

04 / EVIDENCE

Waist ↓ + strength ↑ + endurance ↑ + photos improving = success, even when the scale is stubborn.

Training system

Three purposeful gym sessions. One physical practice you actually enjoy.

DAY A / STRONG

Build the base.

Heavy, controlled compounds. Strength is the foundation.

  • 1
    Squat
    3 × 5–8
  • 2
    Bench press
    3 × 5–8
  • 3
    Pull-ups / pulldown
    3 × 6–10
  • 4
    Romanian deadlift
    3 × 6–10
  • 5
    Hanging knee/leg raise
    3 × 8–15
  • 6
    Farmer carry
    3 hard carries
WHY: Full-body strength + core + grip. This is the foundation of the physique you want.
DAY B / ATHLETIC

Become useful.

Strength that transfers to real movement.

  • 1
    Deadlift / trap-bar
    3 × 4–6
  • 2
    Overhead press
    3 × 5–8
  • 3
    Bulgarian split squat
    3 × 8 / leg
  • 4
    Row
    3 × 8–12
  • 5
    Ab wheel
    3 × 6–12
  • 6
    Sled pushes
    5–8 hard runs
WHY: Unilateral legs, bracing and conditioning turn gym strength into athletic capacity.
DAY C / PHYSIQUE

Shape the result.

Build muscle while conditioning the engine.

  • 1
    Front / hack squat
    3 × 8–10
  • 2
    Incline DB press
    3 × 8–12
  • 3
    Pull-ups
    3 × 6–10
  • 4
    Hip thrust / RDL
    3 × 8–12
  • 5
    Cable crunch
    3 × 10–15
  • 6
    Lateral raises
    3 × 12–20
  • 7
    Cardio
    15–25 min
WHY: Enough hypertrophy to look athletic, direct abs, then cardio to build the engine.
